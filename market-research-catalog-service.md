## 1. Honest take

**Yes — viable as a niche services product, not as a SaaS platform play.** Locofo is selling a *buyer-presentation toolkit*: print-ready A3 one-pagers (improved Haloo-style sell sheets) plus a templated, desktop-first interactive catalog (360/frame-spin, tabbed B2B specs, shortlist, PDF export). That sits in a real gap between Canva/PDF chaos and full wholesale platforms (NuORDER, Adobe Commerce B2B, etc.). Demand for better product presentation is well supported by B2B omnichannel and content research; demand specifically for *agency-built interactive catalogs for China OEM/ODM exporters pitching Western retailers* is under-documented in formal reports — treat China-sourcing presentation norms as adjacent evidence plus field validation.

**Strengths**
- Hybrid print + interactive matches how hardgoods deals still close: trade shows, showroom meetings, WhatsApp/WeChat PDFs, then follow-up links.
- Figma → templated web is a real ops advantage: speed and margin if SKU pages reuse one shell.
- Clear job: help a sourcing/trading company look “retailer-ready” vs Alibaba dump.
- Demo (MH900 spin + Haloo-level PDF) is concrete proof, not vaporware.
- HK position: bilingual/cultural bridge between China factories and US/EU buyer expectations.

**Risks**
- Buyers may confuse you with “build us a shop” or “just host our 360s on Sirv” — wrong ACV and wrong retention.
- Content ops (photos, specs, packing data) is the bottleneck; design alone does not close deals.
- Wholesale platforms own the long-term “shoppable line sheet” budget; Locofo must stay upstream as *presentation / sales-enablement*, not compete on ERP/order.
- Price sensitivity among small traders; enterprise exporters may want in-house or big agency.
- Thin published data on China export catalog spend — validation must be sales-driven, not report-driven.

**Bottom line:** Ship a productized offer (A3 pack + interactive add-on + light retainer), lead with the MH900 demo, sell to trading houses and agents who pitch retailers — not to factories that only need Alibaba images.

## 2. Who buys

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Segment | Who | Why they buy Locofo |
|---|---|---|
| **Sourcing / buying offices** | HK, Shenzhen, Ningbo, Yiwu agents presenting assortments to overseas retailers | Need polished leave-behinds and shareable links for buyer meetings |
| **Trading houses** | Multi-category exporters (home, outdoor, hardgoods, sports, garden) | Seasonal lines change fast; Canva/PDF quality looks cheap vs European competitors |
| **OEM/ODM factories with export sales** | Factories with a sales office pitching private-label / white-label to brands & chains | Need retailer-facing sheets without building a full brand site |
| **Brand exporters / importers** | China-origin brands selling into US/EU wholesale | Want B2B-grade product pages without Shopify storefront chrome |
| **Sales agents / reps** | People who walk Canton Fair / Ambiente / ISPO / private showrooms | Need shortlist + PDF for post-meeting follow-up |

**Primary buyer persona:** Sales director / export manager / merchandising lead at a trading or sourcing firm — not the factory floor, not IT.

**Secondary:** Marketing at mid-size OEM with budget for “catalog refresh” before a fair or retailer pitch season.

### Jobs-to-be-done (when pitching products to buyers/stores)

1. **Make the product look decision-ready** — retailer buyer can see form, finish, key specs, packing, MOQ/logistics in one glance.
2. **Survive the meeting** — leave A3/print or PDF that does not get lost in a folder of Alibaba screenshots.
3. **Enable remote follow-up** — send a link the buyer’s colleague can open on desktop (spin, tabs, shortlist) without a login maze.
4. **Assemble a line** — shortlist SKUs across a session; export a clean PDF pack for internal approval.
5. **Signal professionalism** — look like a supplier who can serve Lidl/Decathlon/Home Depot-class rigor, even if the order is smaller.
6. **Reduce “wrong product” risk** — 360/multi-angle + clear specs cut back-and-forth and returns anxiety (buyer confidence, not DTC conversion).

## 3. Problem landscape — why current catalogs fail

| Status quo | Failure mode |
|---|---|
| **PDF dumps / multi-page line sheets** | Out of date the day price/SKU changes; hard to scan on a call; ugly when generated from Excel. Wholesale blogs still describe static PDFs as the default pain. |
| **Alibaba / Made-in-China screenshots** | Looks like a commodity trader; missing packing, retail-facing benefits, consistent photography; weak trust with US/EU category buyers. |
| **Static spreadsheets + phone photos** | Specs exist; sales story does not. Buyers cannot visualize finish, ventilation, hinge, packing cube. |
| **Canva sell sheets** | Fast but generic; inconsistent brand system; rarely print-bleed / A3 production-ready; logistics blocks often missing. |
| **Full photographer turntables + agency microsites** | Excellent quality; slow and expensive per SKU; overkill for a 20-SKU fair pack. |
| **Sirv / Cloudimage / 360 SaaS alone** | Solves spin hosting; does not solve layout, B2B info architecture, shortlist, PDF, or sales narrative. |
| **Generic agency websites / Shopify** | Cart, reviews, SEO chrome distracts; wrong mental model (storefront vs presentation). Overbuilt and slow to update for seasonal assortments. |
| **China catalog aggregators / giant PDF catalogues** | Prove catalogs are still the currency of sourcing — but quality is factory-brochure, not retailer-pitch. |

**Core insight:** The gap is not “more pixels.” It is *sales-enablement design* for the moment a China-side seller presents hardgoods to a Western retail buyer — hybrid print + interactive, logistics-aware, shortlistable.

## 5. Market signals & studies

*Prefer reputable publishers. Vendor case studies and industry blogs noted as soft where relevant. China-specific presentation spend: thin — see gap note.*

### B2B digital catalogs / content commerce / wholesale digitization

1. **NuORDER by Lightspeed — “How to Digitize Your Wholesale Business” (blog, citing NuORDER 2026 State of B2B eCommerce Report)**  
   - URL: https://blog.nuorder.com/how-to-digitize-wholesale-business  
   - Takeaway: Wholesale leaders still fighting static PDFs/line sheets; digital catalogs + virtual showrooms (incl. HD/360) are a core pillar; blog cites **78% of senior wholesale leaders** ranking B2B eCommerce as #1 investment priority (vendor survey — treat as directional).

2. **McKinsey — “Five fundamental truths: How B2B winners keep growing” (B2B Pulse 2024, Sep 2024)**  
   - URL: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/five-fundamental-truths-how-b2b-winners-keep-growing  
   - Takeaway: Among orgs that offer e-commerce, it is now the **top revenue channel (~34% of revenue)**; buyers use **~10 interaction channels**; **rule of thirds** (in-person / remote / digital self-serve) is stable — supports hybrid print + interactive presentation, not digital-only.

3. **Salesforce — “5 B2B Commerce Trends Already Emerging in 2024” (Feb 2024)**  
   - URL: https://www.salesforce.com/blog/best-b2b-commerce-trends/  
   - Takeaway: B2B firms expect **>50% of revenue from digital channels within two years** (vs ~30% digitally driven the prior year in their framing); **63% of B2B buyers** say experiences fall short of what they know is possible — room for better product presentation UX.

4. **Deloitte Digital — “Elevating B2B commerce with composable architecture” (Apr 2025)**  
   - URL: https://www.deloittedigital.com/us/en/insights/perspective/b2b-composable-commerce.html

5. **BigCommerce — Global B2B Buyer Behavior Report (survey May 2023; press Sep 2023)**

## 6. Pricing / packaging hypotheses (not final quotes)

*Anchors: HK catalogue design market rates (PRO360, updated Mar 2025) show product catalog design roughly **HK$180–800/page** for ~10-page jobs and lower per-page at higher volume; cover **HK$800–2,000**; photography often separate. Use as floor/ceiling context, not Locofo MSRP.*  
Source: https://www.pro360.com.hk/zh-hk/price/catelog_design

### Recommended packaging (hypotheses)

| Package | What’s in it | Hypothesis price band (HKD) | Logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A3 Pack** | 5–20 print-ready A3 horizontal sell sheets (Haloo-improved: hero, callouts, dense specs, packing/logistics block); Figma source + print PDF | **HK$8,000–25,000** per pack (or **HK$1,200–2,500 / sheet** at low volume) | Above DIY Canva; below multi-page book design; priced per *meeting asset*, not per booklet page |
| **Interactive add-on** | Desktop-first product pages from same content system: 360/frame-spin, tabs, shortlist, PDF export; hosted link for buyer share | **HK$15,000–40,000** setup for template + first 5–15 SKUs; then **HK$800–2,000 / SKU** | High margin if one Figma→web shell; spin frames from existing photo set |
| **Seasonal retainer** | Monthly SKU updates, new sheets, fair sprint support, light copy/spec QA | **HK$8,000–20,000 / month** (3–6 month seasons) | Matches Canton Fair / Ambiente / retailer pitch calendar |

**Why interactive is high-margin if templated**
- One IA/shell (media left, tabs, shortlist drawer, PDF mapper) amortizes design + frontend.
- Per-SKU cost collapses to: photo normalize → spin sequence → spec fields → QA.
- Hosting can be static/cheap; avoid building cart/ERP.
- Upsell path: more SKUs, bilingual, private password link, co-branded agent editions.

**Do not** price like Shopify builds or NuORDER subscriptions — different job, different buyer.

## 7. Go-to-market angles (HK / APAC → sourcing firms)

1. **Fair-season sprints** — “20 SKUs interactive + A3 pack in 3 weeks before Canton Fair / Ambiente / ISPO.” Deadline selling.
2. **Retailer-pitch upgrade** — Message to agents: *stop sending Alibaba screenshots to category buyers.* Show MH900 demo side-by-side with a typical factory PDF.
3. **Partner through sourcing agencies** — White-label Locofo pages inside their client presentations; they resell as value-add.
4. **HK showroom / trading office lunch-and-learn** — 20-min demo: spin → shortlist → PDF export on a real hardgood.
5. **Category wedges** — Start where look/feel matters and 360 helps: outdoor/sports (sunglasses demo), home hardgoods, garden, small appliances — not pure commodities (screws).
6. **Bilingual production** — EN sheets for US/EU buyers; CN working files for factory data collection — Locofo as process, not just pixels.
7. **Avoid** competing with Alibaba store decoration or full B2B portal RFPs.

## 8. Open questions / validation experiments (5 cheap tests)

1. **10 LinkedIn / WhatsApp cold demos** — Send MH900 interactive link + 1 A3 PDF to export managers at HK/SZ trading houses; track reply rate and “would you pay for 20 SKUs?”  
   *Pass:* ≥3 of 10 book a call.

2. **Price card test** — In those calls, offer three bands (A3-only / interactive add-on / retainer) without discounting; note which they choose and flinch point.

3. **Fair floor intercept** — At one HK or SZ trade event, collect 15 business cards from people who currently use PDF catalogs; ask “what’s broken?” in one sentence; offer free redesign of *one* SKU as lead magnet.

4. **Competitor teardown sprint (1 day)** — Document 5 real China exporter catalogs (PDF) + 2 Western brand line sheets; score on specs, packing, photography, shareability. Use as sales leave-behind.

5. **Ops time study** — Produce 5 SKUs end-to-end from Figma + photos; measure hours per SKU. If >X hours, redesign template before selling retainers (protect margin).
